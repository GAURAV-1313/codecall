Points to remember

  • Order of app.use() calls is the order requests are filtered.
  • A route added later is unprotected unless it sits under the guarded prefix.
  • Verify the token, then load the user; never the reverse.

Before reusing this pattern elsewhere

  • Health and webhook endpoints usually need to bypass the gate on purpose.
  • Clock skew breaks expiry checks across machines; allow a small leeway.
